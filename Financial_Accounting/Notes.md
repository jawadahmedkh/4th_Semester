## 1. Business Transactions

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A["Is it a person / firm / bank / creditor / debtor / outstanding / prepaid / accrued / received-in-advance?"] -->|Yes| B["Personal Account"]
    B --> B1["Natural Person"]
    B --> B2["Artificial Person"]
    B --> B3["Representative Personal\n(e.g. Outstanding Rent)"]

    A -->|No| C["Is it something the business owns or a right\n(can be seen or measured)?"]
    C -->|Yes| D["Real Account"]
    D --> D1["Tangible Asset\n(Land, Machine, Stock)"]
    D --> D2["Intangible Asset\n(Goodwill, Patent)"]

    C -->|No| E["Nominal Account\n(Incomes, Expenses, Gains, Losses)"]

    E --> F["Special / Contra / Valuation Accounts\n(Provision for Depreciation, Provision for Doubtful Debts, Stock Reserve, Bills Discounted)"]
    F --> G["Treat as Valuation / Contra Account\n(Modify Main Account)"]

```
